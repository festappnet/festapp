Start with the saved public form link, then check the published website.

The form does not load

Open its public URL separately. It must contain /form/ and the saved form identifier. Do not use an administration address. Check the sales period and form availability.

Content or the submit button is cut off

For automatic height, keep the complete code, including the helper script. Check whether your website removed it. If scripts are unsupported, choose fixed height and check the longer order summary on mobile.

The colors or font look wrong

Turn off the inherit-style setting and compare the result. Check light and dark backgrounds, including prices, totals and form controls.

An older version is showing

Save both the form and the website page. Clear your website or WordPress cache, then check the published page in a private browser window.

Contact support

Send the published page URL, the public form URL, a screenshot and the steps that reproduce the problem. Include your website builder and height setting. Do not send passwords, access tokens or buyers' personal details. Contact support.
