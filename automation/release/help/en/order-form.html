Choose what buyers fill in, what they can buy and when ordering is available. Finish by checking the saved public link.

Before you begin

You need an event and permission to edit its forms. Prepare the ticket offer and payment setup for paid orders.

Open or create a form

Open Forms in the event administration and select a form or create one. The form tab edits fields and content, settings control the address and availability, and Design controls appearance.

Build the buyer's form

  1. Check contact fields

    Keep the email and organizer-required buyer details. Collect only information you need. Required fields must be filled before the buyer can continue.

  2. Add tickets and the offer

    Add a ticket field and connect the appropriate products, prices and optional extras. Follow prices and capacity for the offer itself.

  3. Add your questions

    Use Add Field for a note or other question. A question for the whole order differs from one inside each ticket. Visibility and required status are separate settings.

  4. Review the form

    Check field order, labels and prices. Hide unnecessary options. Products or fields already used by orders may be protected from deletion.

Save and copy the public link

Save the form, then copy its current saved public link containing /form/. An unsaved address preview is not a confirmed public URL. If an address is already taken, choose another and save again.

Set ordering availability

Check sales start and end times and any manual closure. Before opening, visitors may see a countdown and introductory information. Publishing an event listing does not itself open the form.

Check as a visitor

Open the public link in a private window on desktop and mobile. Select a ticket, fill required fields and verify the order summary and total. You can check appearance without submitting; submission can reserve tickets and send an email. Then embed the form or share the direct link.
