See where visitors come from and where they complete an order. First choose whether your website or the organizer's container owns measurement. Checkout works without optional consent.

Choose where measurement runs

Your website’s dataLayer sends embedded form events to your website's dataLayer. Your website owns GA4, Google Ads, Meta and consent. Use the website's existing measurement destinations to connect its visit with checkout. Saved embeds keep their selected measurement destination. Choose website dataLayer measurement in the embed generator before copying the code.

The organizer’s GTM in Festapp runs the organizer's configured GTM container or scripts inside an isolated measurement frame. It uses the form's consent notice or explicitly enabled website consent transfer. Standalone forms use the organizer’s GTM in Festapp. Browsers separate the frame's storage from your website: matching G-IDs do not guarantee a shared visit or campaign source. Festapp does not copy GA, Ads or Meta cookies or extend their lifetime.

For the most accurate measurement, we recommend embedding the form directly on your website with events sent to your website’s dataLayer. Measurement then runs in your website's GTM and your banner controls consent, so the visit source and conversions match the rest of your website measurement. A standalone form link uses the form's own consent notice. Taking over consent from your website banner is supported for selected banners (including CookieConsent v3 by orestbida and Cookiebot). We try to keep this support up to date, but after your website banner changes or updates it may take some time before the takeover works again. Without a usable choice from the website banner, consent stays denied in the embedded form. A standalone form can show its own notice.

Enable measurement for the organizer and form

  1. Open the organizer's settings and Measurement. Enable measurement for the organizer, enter the Google Tag Manager container ID such as GTM-XXXX, and a full HTTPS Privacy policy URL. Enter the container ID rather than installation code.{{APP_LINK:organization}}
  2. Choose the cookie notice layout and optional custom title/text. Default wording follows the visitor's language; custom wording is shared across languages. Configure Custom scripts (advanced) with your measurement administrator and appropriate consent gates.
  3. If the organizer still needs platform approval, use Request measurement approval under Basics. The request covers initial permission to send events to your website’s dataLayer or run the organizer’s GTM in Festapp. The date appears after you send it. The platform administrator handles the request manually and replies by email. Contact support if the request cannot be sent.
  4. Open the saved form, enable its Allow measurement setting and save. Enable each form separately. Its status explains disabled measurement, missing destination/privacy policy, platform restrictions or an unverified runtime.{{APP_LINK:forms}}

Add your websites

Under Websites, enter the exact HTTPS address including any www, without a path or trailing slash. Select Add and save the organizer settings. You can add up to 10 websites you operate. Once the platform approves measurement for the organizer, all saved websites are eligible automatically.

Visitor consent

When sending events to your website’s dataLayer, establish denied defaults before tags and let your CMP update consent after a saved choice. Tags without native Consent Mode support, including Meta Pixel, Clarity and custom HTML, need explicit consent checks in your tag manager. Loading GTM or receiving an event does not itself authorize cookies or advertising data sharing.

Import the configuration exported from organizer settings

First add and save your website. Turn on Use consent from the website cookie banner, confirm the terms, and save the organizer settings. Select Edit on the website card and choose the platform and banner.

  1. Select your website banner. For banners with a policy ID, the policy ID is automatically read from your saved website. Review it and confirm coverage of the form. Use Reload to retry. If detection fails, expand Enter manually and enter the policy ID. If the ID changes before confirmation, reload and confirm the current version. CookieConsent v3 offers optional category mapping, defaulting to analytics and marketing. For ordinary measurement, Cookiebot and CookieConsent use a validity window starting at coverage confirmation, set by the server. After confirming and importing the export, require a new visitor choice in your banner. A choice saved before confirmation is not imported. Repeat this after a scope change; checkout remains available.
  2. Explicitly confirm that the website notice covers the form, measurement purposes, recipients, organizer measurement on *.pages.dev, Festapp as processor and withdrawal. Select Confirm settings, then Copy code for … on the website card. Unsaved settings, unavailable server state or an invalid binding cannot provide a usable export.
  3. If your website plugin has an import field, paste the code into Configuration exported from Festapp, select Enable imported configuration and save. If the widget settings are in Czech, use Konfigurace exportovaná z Festappu, select Zapnout importovanou konfiguraci and publish. For HTML and Webnode, open Embed on your website, enter the saved public form link, paste the configuration into Configuration exported from Festapp and enable import. Replace the entire HTML block with the generated code and publish the page.

Do not manually copy internal organization or organizer IDs, internal consent revisions, or edit the export. The server owns scope and current binding state. After a policy or mapping change, confirm coverage again and replace the export. Disabling the binding in organizer settings also invalidates previously issued exports.

Transfer uses the embeds and links created by the extension, widget or generator; manual links remain untouched. It requires a saved banner choice. Defaults, missing, outdated or withdrawn choices, unavailable policy and incompatible COOP window isolation leave consent denied in the embedded form. Checkout remains available. An explicit choice made in the standalone form takes precedence for frames and links. Withdrawal on the website is synchronized; closing the website, source disconnection or expiry of the short renewable connection revokes the import. Imported choices last only for this visit, at most 24 hours; reloads require fresh transfer. A saved local choice lasts 180 days and can be changed through Consent settings. Cookie choice transfer does not preserve analytics visitor identity.

Configure GTM tags

Download the paused website GTM preset. In a new workspace, use Merge and Rename conflicting tags, triggers and variables. All 18 tags in the Festapp folder are paused. Fill the GA4 ID, GAds ID, the Google Ads purchase and registration conversion labels and FB Pixel ID constants. Keep the website's Google tag and Conversion Linker. The official Meta template loads and initializes the pixel itself; verify its integration with the existing pixel and no duplicate views or conversions. This preset belongs in the website container, not the isolated Unit container.

Each trigger requires form_embedded = true and a specific form event. GA4 separates ecommerce and generic events to avoid inheriting a previous website event's ecommerce state. Keep form_page_view distinct from the native website page_view. Remove overlapping conversion tags before enabling these. In an existing container, select the form’s Purchase timing and remove duplicate Purchase tags.

What your notice and privacy policy should describe

Describe the organizer as controller, Festapp as processor, measurement and advertising purposes, the actual services/recipients, and any sharing of order email/phone with advertising systems. Include the isolated *.pages.dev measurement domain when using the organizer’s GTM in Festapp. Specify retention and further transfers according to your actual service configuration. For transferred consent, explicitly cover form purchases, purposes, validity and withdrawal both on the website and in the form. Retain the relevant notice wording and saved decision revision; changing the wording does not update an old consent record.

Purchase timing is configured per form: order (default, including unpaid orders) or paid (eligible full bank or online payment). Meta Conversions API requires paid mode. The preset includes GA4 purchase, Ads Purchase and Meta Purchase, with Meta eventID from ecommerce.transaction_id, matching CAPI. Paid browser events require a return to the authorized overview and current visitor policy and consent. General GA4/Ads Purchase remains independent of advertising consent and CAPI success. Standalone overview events require separate triggers for form_embedded=false in the isolated container.

Verify and troubleshoot

  • No measurement: check organization, organizer and form enablement, platform approval, destination, HTTPS privacy policy and the exact saved website address. When sending events to your website’s dataLayer, check the helper script and dataLayer events.
  • Consent stays denied: check the saved CMP record, every advertising permission and the transfer switch. For links, also check the configured adapter, saved choice and matching CMP policy/form scope. Do not bypass a refusal with granted defaults.
  • Your own website appears as a referral: use website mode for embeds and the native website's destinations. Normal GA4 cross-domain settings and _gl apply to supported navigation between those domains; they do not bypass frame storage isolation. Cookie copying and automatic identity transfer into the Unit runtime are unsupported.
  • Before publishing: use GTM Preview / Tag Assistant to check denied, granted and withdrawn consent, one approved test order, value, currency and order ID. Preview has limitations inside the isolated Unit frame. A successful import proves neither conversion delivery nor campaign attribution: verify both in your actual destination account. Unpause reviewed tags only after those checks.

Meta Conversions API

Organizer managers and organization administrators can open organizer settings → Events and consent → Meta Conversions API. Enter the dataset ID and access token, choose a saved website and save with sending enabled. Set up website consent and enable measurement and Meta CAPI on each form you want to track.

In Meta Events Manager, select the dataset → Settings → Conversions API → manual setup → Generate access token. Paste the token into Access token and save; its full saved value is never displayed. To tell tokens apart, the field shows only its last ten characters and the date it was saved. Copy the server test event code from the same dataset's Test events tab. While this code is filled in, purchases go only to the test. Clear and save the code after testing. See Meta's setup guide.

Paused means a token error. Save a replacement token to resume. Orders already failed after six attempts are never retried; pending orders are eligible only within seven days of full payment. The section shows 30-day counts for pending, sent, failed, revoked, invalid and expired deliveries. Terminal states use completion time; pending deliveries use consent capture time. The last reason also explains connection changes, invalid consent, an ineligible order, payment older than seven days, exhausted attempts or expiry of the 30-day consent retention period. These terminal outcomes are never retried and reasons contain no personal data. Changing the dataset, website or test code invalidates earlier pending deliveries. Disconnecting requires confirmation and erases the token and matching data. A delivery already in flight retains only its receipt fence and records a successful result as sent_in_flight. The last error distinguishes a scheduled retry from a final failure with no more attempts. Turning off the connector immediately blocks capture and preparation.

Forms with Meta CAPI enabled use the saved advertising choice from the existing cookie banner, without another checkbox. The order stores all three advertising permissions, the current consent revision and a server capture time; consent imported from a website also includes its website binding. All supported banners and the standalone notice use this model. A decision date is optional; an older saved choice does not need to be saved again for CAPI. Your banner and privacy policy must cover advertising through Meta. Later payments can then use the consent captured with the order. Customers can withdraw through their order overview. Withdrawal stops deliveries that the server has not yet prepared. A prepared event may still reach Meta after withdrawal. If Meta confirms receipt, it is recorded as sent_in_flight. Closing the tab normally does not withdraw the saved order choice.

For CAPI-enabled orders, the general Purchase remains available to GA4/Ads. Pixel uses a separate meta_purchase companion only after server delivery succeeds and within 47 hours of full payment; it never fires GA4/Ads tags. This prevents a browser event from preceding a server retry outside the duplicate window. Changing settings applies to new orders; it does not change the event identity of existing orders.
