Prepare and save your order form first. You can then share its public link or embed it in your website.

  1. Sign in and prepare your event

    Open the administration. Register if you do not have an account. Provide the event and organizer details your visitors need before purchasing.

  2. Prepare your tickets

    Check ticket names, prices, capacity and sales dates. For assigned seating, prepare the seating plan and connect its seats to the ticket offer. Set up the payment methods for your event.

  3. Save the order form

    Choose the details you need from each buyer and save the form. Use its public link, containing /form/, rather than the address of an administration screen.

  4. Open the public link

    Check it in a private browser window. Verify the event name, ticket offer, prices and required fields. Outside the sales period the page may show an availability message.

    Submitting an actual order can reserve tickets and send a confirmation email. Do not submit an order just to check the page appearance.

  5. Choose where to sell

    Share the link directly or continue with HTML embedding, WordPress or Webnode.
