Find an order, distinguish payment from email delivery and fix a customer's email without creating a duplicate purchase.

Before you begin

You need access to orders for the correct event and edit permission to make changes. Ask for the order symbol or contact email. Customer passwords and bank-login details are not needed.

Find and inspect the order

  1. Open Orders in the correct event

    Check the event name in navigation. Search by order symbol, email or name. Review filters and cancelled items if the order is missing.{{APP_LINK:orders}}

  2. Read the detail and history

    Compare the contact, tickets, seats, total, amount paid and remaining balance. History distinguishes the original purchase from later changes.

  3. Separate the states

    An order may exist without being paid. A paid order may still have an email delivery failure. Sending does not prove that the customer read the message. Admission is a separate ticket state.

Repair contact and delivery

Open delivery recovery from the email warning and inspect delivery details. Verify the address with the customer. Saving only updates the contact without sending a new message. Use the offered save-and-send action for tickets or confirmation according to the current order state.

Before retrying, check whether sending is queued, blocked or uncertain. Queued is not confirmed delivery. Fix a blocked recipient or ask an administrator to review it instead of repeatedly sending the same message.

Change or cancel an order

After changing products or seats, check the new price and balance. Choosing a customer update sends a real email. Cancellation does not itself refund money; handle a refund separately through the supported payment process and verify its result.

Check the sales summary

Refresh after handling the order. Report provides a sales summary; it does not replace one transaction's detail or recipient delivery history.

Verify the resolution

Check the saved contact, actual payment balance and current delivery result. Ask the customer to check spam too. Do not send another person's order or create another purchase merely to replace a missing confirmation.
