Check the receiving account, prepare payment instructions and deadlines, and check what the buyer sees after ordering.

Before you begin

Prepare paid products, their currencies and a bank account linked to the organization. Managing bank accounts requires the appropriate permissions. This bank account is separate from a customer's login account.

Check the receiving account for an existing form

First prepare the receiving account using Bank account setup. Fio synchronization has a separate API token and connection guide. This guide changes payment instructions for an existing form, not the account at the bank.

Open Forms → the form → Settings → Payment Details. For each currency, the app uses the first organizer account supporting it.{{APP_LINK:forms}}

To change that choice, edit account priority and supported currencies in the organizer's Bank Accounts. This affects new orders across its forms; check existing orders in order detail → Payments.

The initial account check for a new event belongs to the separate setup wizard.

Set instructions and deadlines

Check payment details, reference generation, recipient message and payment deadline. Choose inherited event settings or a form-specific value. Review reminders and the reply-to contact.

An unpaid order does not automatically cancel or release its seats after the deadline. The organizer handles cancellation. A reminder is not confirmation that money was received.

Check the buyer's instructions

After deliberately submitting a test order, check the amount, currency, account, reference and payment QR. The payment-QR setting controls instructions on the confirmation screen. An accepted order is not a paid order. Submission may reserve capacity and send an email, so resolve the test order afterward.

Wrong or missing payment reference

Follow manual bank payment matching to find a transfer, check permissions and correct a link to another order.

If a payment is not matched

Open order detail → Payments and compare the balance with the received transaction's amount and reference, and check bank connection status. Verify the first payment before asking a customer to pay again. Use other payment methods only when they are actually activated and displayed for your organization.{{APP_LINK:orders}}

Record cash actually received

Open the order detail, choose Payments, then Add Payment. Enter the amount actually received and confirm. This records cash, not a bank transfer. For a transfer, follow bank payment matching. Recheck the received amount and balance in the order detail.
