Select the receiving account, prepare payment instructions and deadlines, and check what the buyer sees after ordering.

Before you begin

Prepare paid products, their currencies and a bank account linked to the organization. Managing bank accounts requires the appropriate permissions. This bank account is separate from a customer's login account.

Prepare the receiving account

  1. Check Bank accounts

    Open the organization's bank accounts. An authorized manager can create an account or link an existing one. Check its number, supported currencies and recipient.{{APP_LINK:settings}}

  2. Select the account for sales

    Choose and confirm the receiving account in event setup. For an existing form, verify its payment settings and currency routing. If no suitable account is available, save the draft and ask the manager to prepare one.

  3. Check the bank connection

    For automatic matching, verify the bank connection status. A stored token or forwarding address alone does not prove that the connection is verified and working. Without it, use the supported manual payment-recording process.

Set instructions and deadlines

Check payment details, reference generation, recipient message and payment deadline. Choose inherited event settings or a form-specific value. Review reminders and the reply-to contact.

An unpaid order does not automatically cancel or release its seats after the deadline. The organizer handles cancellation. A reminder is not confirmation that money was received.

Check the buyer's instructions

After deliberately submitting a test order, check the amount, currency, account, reference and payment QR. The payment-QR setting controls instructions on the confirmation screen. An accepted order is not a paid order. Submission may reserve capacity and send an email, so resolve the test order afterward.

If a payment is not matched

Compare the order balance with the received transaction's amount and reference, and check bank connection status. Verify the first payment before asking a customer to pay again. Use other payment methods only when they are actually activated and displayed for your organization.
