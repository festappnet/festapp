Set clear ticket variants, prices and availability. Your limits must match what you can actually sell.

Before you begin

You need a form and permission to edit its offer. Prepare variant names, prices, currencies and quantities. Decide which variants share the same physical capacity.

Prepare products and prices

  1. Open the form offer

    A product type groups related choices, such as admission or dinner. A product is one priced choice, such as standard admission or a vegetarian dinner.

  2. Add the choices

    Use Add Product Type and Add Product. Enter a clear name, price and currency. A zero price means a free option, not zero availability.

  3. Check selection rules

    Choose whether selection is required and whether multiple choices are allowed. Enable Show only for options you want to offer. Check any preselected choice.

Set the available quantity

Set a quantity or capacity for limited products. Where the field explicitly says that zero means unlimited, do not use zero to close sales; use visibility or availability instead. Do not apply this rule to a standing area's seat count.

When different price variants consume the same stock, put them in the same capacity group. Two separate limits of 100 do not create one shared limit of 100. For assigned seating, also check each seat's product binding.

Changes during sales

Save price or availability changes and check the public form. Changing the offer does not automatically rewrite existing order prices. Edit an existing order separately and check its balance. Use scheduled price changes only when a change is intended for a specific future time.

Verify the result

Check every visible option, currency and total in a private window. Availability can depend on the sales period, shared capacity and current holds. Check existing reservations before increasing limits to resolve an unavailable option.
