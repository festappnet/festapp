# Support - Festapp Tickets

## Technical support

Email `info@festapp.net` with the page where the problem occurred, your browser and a short description of the steps. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, complete ticket QR codes or one-time account deletion links.

## Contact the event organizer

The organizer handles questions about an event, registration, orders, payments, tickets, cancellations and refunds. Use the contact shown with the event or order.
