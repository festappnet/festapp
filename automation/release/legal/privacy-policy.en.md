## Data we use

Accounts require a first name, last name and email address. Depending on the registration or order, we also process contact and billing details, form answers, participant details, tickets and payment records. Organizers may request additional information such as a birth date. Required fields are marked; without them a service may be unavailable.

Data comes from you, the person placing the order, a legal representative, the organizer or payment records. Security records may include IP addresses and request times.

## Why we use data

We use data for accounts, registrations, orders, payments, communication and support. The legal basis is performance of a contract, steps before a contract, a legal obligation or legitimate interests in security and protecting rights. Optional processing may require separate consent. Registration does not consent to advertising or optional tracking.

## Payments

Card payments use Stripe Checkout and the organizer’s connected Stripe account. Stripe processes payment details under its own applicable terms and privacy information. The application records payment status and financial references needed for the order. Bank transfer details are used when the organizer enables that method.

## Email

We send account and order messages, including sign-in links, confirmations, tickets and organizer information. The existing delivery service uses Amazon SES in Frankfurt, processing recipient addresses, messages and attachments.

Delivery and errors are recorded. Some order and organizer messages measure opening through an image associated with the recipient and loading time. Security messages do not use this measurement.

## Optional connection to ChatGPT

If you connect your account to OpenAI's ChatGPT, you select events or organizers and approve the access scope. The connector returns data for your requested operation within that scope and your current permissions. Read access supports event discovery, sales summaries, order search and details, and ticket artwork. It does not create orders, change payments or deliver tickets.

The public ChatGPT connector returns event names and identifiers, order identifiers and symbols, order and ticket counts and states, amounts, currencies, payment totals and ticket artwork configuration. It withholds buyer names, surnames, email addresses, notes, registration responses and form-field definitions from orders. Search supports the exact order symbol, not contact details. Identifiers and symbols are not anonymous data. The connector is intended for organizers and must not be used to process children’s data or government identifiers. Do not include such data in event names or ticket artwork. Returned content can become part of your conversation. Include only data you are entitled to disclose for that purpose.

OpenAI processes returned data under the terms and settings of your ChatGPT account. Read-only access still involves data transfer. Connecting is optional; the service remains usable without it. You can revoke access in your account's connection settings. Revocation stops future connector access but does not itself delete earlier ChatGPT conversation content, which you manage with OpenAI.

## Where data is processed

The main database and accounts run on self-managed Hetzner servers. Cloudflare provides website delivery, security, files and encrypted backups. Organizers and their authorized staff have access as needed.

Optional Google sign-in provides an account identifier, verified email and name. Google Fonts supplies fonts and icons. Online maps may load OpenStreetMap resources. These providers receive IP addresses and request information. Browser location requires permission.

Some providers have global infrastructure. Transfers outside the European Economic Area require appropriate safeguards, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. Ask the contact address for details.

Aggregate browser measurement of public pages is described below. Hosting and security services process requests needed to deliver and protect the website. Server records may include IP addresses, requested paths and technical information. These records are not described as anonymous. ## Retention

Account data is retained while the account is used. Registrations and orders remain as needed for the event, settlement and complaints, and longer where law or protection of legal claims requires it. The organizer determines event-specific retention. Email records support delivery, troubleshooting and evidence of the service. Deleted data may temporarily remain in older backups.
