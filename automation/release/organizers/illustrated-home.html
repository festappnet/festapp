How much does it cost?

Pricing is agreed for your event. Guest bank transfers go directly to your account; the service fee is handled separately. Card payments are available by arrangement.

Can I offer reserved seats and dinner?

Yes. Create your own seating plan and offer guests specific seats and dinner options.

How do guests receive their tickets?

Guests receive order and ticket information by email, based on the confirmed order and payment status.

What do I need at the door?

A phone with a camera and access to your event check-in. Scan the ticket and confirm admission.
