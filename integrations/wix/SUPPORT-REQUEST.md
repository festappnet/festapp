## What was resolved without contacting anyone

- Free Premium development sites and our dedicated free static hosting are available.
- Standard revenue sharing is documented; do not ask support to repeat the rates.
- Wix usage-based billing can have additional charges only and no monthly base fee.
- The live app Pricing page explicitly requires App Market approval for custom
  pay-per-use billing and links to the official developer support flow.
- Partner Billed App approval is individual; a generic public page cannot grant it.
