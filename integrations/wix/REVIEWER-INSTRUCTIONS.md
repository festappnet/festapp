# Wix reviewer access

App: vstupenky.online (`9c099206-ce3d-43de-bd9d-deb96bb4f51e`).

Use this dedicated free test form:
https://vstupenky.online/form/wix-review-test-2027

No vstupenky.online account is needed to view the form or complete a free test
order. This event is hidden from the public event catalogue. Its tickets cost
0 CZK and have no admission value for a real event. No bank account or seating
plan is connected.

1. Add the app widget to a Wix page and open its Settings.
2. Paste the test form URL into “Odkaz formuláře” and save.
3. Publish the Wix site. Editor and Preview intentionally show a placeholder;
   the ticket form loads on the published page.
4. Select the free test ticket, enter an email address you control, and continue
   to the summary. You may complete a free test order. No payment details are
   required. Confirmation and test tickets are handled by vstupenky.online.
