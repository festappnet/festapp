2. The seven-day expiry is enforced by the OAuth server's immutable delegation, not a connector preference. Refresh cannot extend it. A fresh refresh token is returned on rotation; a defensive fallback is now present too. Two concurrent real Make scenarios previously refreshed successfully without changing server delegation expiry. Users are explicitly told to reconnect after seven days or revocation.
3. access.inspect returns the complete consent-filtered list, with no pagination contract. The RPC limit bounds its option sample; the list module applies its total bundle limit.

5. The pagination headers enforce technical completion bounds; an empty total means no user limit within those bounds. They do not grant access. A later-page failure is explicit, and no external rollback is promised.
6. The pagination body must retain the event, limit and filters: Make's official page says the body overrides the parent regardless of mergeWithParent. Header/query merging is different. We retained the body and freshly verified all 52 orders over two pages.
   https://developers.make.com/custom-apps-documentation/component-blocks/api/pagination#body
