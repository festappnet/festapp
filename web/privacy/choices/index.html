Your choices and rights - Festapp Tickets

Account management

Use your profile to review your details, manage registrations and start account deletion. The time-limited, one-time email link does not delete anything by itself. Deletion starts after your final confirmation.

After deletion

After confirmation the account is blocked. Authentication, the private profile and related data that need not be retained are removed. Records required by law or to protect legal claims may remain to the necessary extent.

Your rights

Request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent or an objection at info@festapp.net. Contact the organizer as well for event data.

Website traffic

The English profile does not include a browser analytics script. Hosting and security providers process requests needed to deliver and protect the site. Server records can include IP addresses, paths and technical request data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests at info@festapp.net.

Further information

Account registration is not consent to a newsletter or optional tracking. You can remove saved sign-in details, preferences and cached data in your browser settings; doing so may sign you out.

See https://tickets.festap.net/privacy/. Complete an account deletion already started at https://tickets.festap.net/delete-account/.
